About Sydney Opera House

The Sydney Opera House is one of the most distinctive buildings in the world, known for its unique shell-shaped design. Opened in 1973, it is a multipurpose performing arts center located in Sydney Harbour and a symbol of modern architecture.

Enjoy world-class opera, theatre, or ballet performances.

Capture stunning views of the harbour from this iconic location.
