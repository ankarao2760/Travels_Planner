Sydney Harbour Bridge

An iconic steel arch bridge connecting Sydney’s northern and southern shores.

About Sydney Harbour Bridge

The Sydney Harbour Bridge, affectionately known as "The Coathanger," is a heritage-listed steel through arch bridge. It offers pedestrians, cyclists, and vehicles a spectacular view of Sydney Harbour and the Opera House.

Things to Do at Sydney Harbour Bridge

Bridge Climb

Climb the bridge for panoramic views of the city and harbour.

Harbour Walk

Take a stroll across the pedestrian pathway for stunning vistas.

Scenic Views

Enjoy spectacular photo opportunities from various lookout points.
