About Tower Bridge

Tower Bridge is a combined bascule and suspension bridge in London, built between 1886 and 1894. It connects the Tower of London on the north bank to Southwark on the south. The bridge has become an iconic symbol of London and offers stunning views of the city.

Things to Do at Tower Bridge

Walk along the glass walkway for a unique view of the city below.

Explore the Engine Rooms

Learn about the Victorian engineering marvel behind Tower Bridge.

Nighttime Views

Admire the beautifully lit bridge against the London skyline at night.
