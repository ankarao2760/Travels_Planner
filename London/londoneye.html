About the London Eye

The London Eye, also known as the Millennium Wheel, is one of the world’s tallest observation wheels. Opened in 2000, it has become an iconic part of the London skyline and a popular tourist attraction.

Things to Do at the London Eye

Panoramic Views

Enjoy stunning views of London from a height of 135 meters.

Private Capsule

Book a private capsule for a unique and personalized experience.

Evening Rides

Experience the magical London skyline illuminated at night.
