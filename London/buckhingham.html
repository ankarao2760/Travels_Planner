About Buckingham Palace

Buckingham Palace is the London residence and administrative headquarters of the British monarch. Built in 1703 as a townhouse, it became the royal residence in 1837. The Palace is renowned for its beautiful gardens and iconic Changing of the Guard ceremony.

Things to Do at Buckingham Palace

Changing of the Guard

Witness this iconic ceremony outside the palace gates.

Tour the State Rooms

Explore the grand state rooms during the annual summer opening.

Palace Gardens

Stroll through the stunning gardens surrounding the palace.
