Senso-ji Temple

Tokyo's oldest and most famous Buddhist temple, a must-visit historical site.

About Senso-ji Temple

Senso-ji Temple, located in Asakusa, Tokyo, is the oldest temple in the city, founded in 628 AD. This Buddhist temple is dedicated to the bodhisattva Kannon and attracts millions of visitors every year. The temple complex includes the famous Kaminarimon Gate, which is a symbol of Tokyo, and the shopping street of Nakamise, filled with traditional Japanese goods and snacks.

Things to Do at Senso-ji Temple

Visit Kaminarimon Gate

Take a photo at the iconic Kaminarimon Gate, the entrance to Senso-ji Temple.

Explore Nakamise Street

Walk through Nakamise, a lively shopping street leading to the temple with traditional Japanese treats.

Pray at the Main Hall

Offer your prayers and take in the peaceful atmosphere at the Main Hall of Senso-ji Temple.
