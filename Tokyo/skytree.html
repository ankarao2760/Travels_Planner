The Tokyo Skytree

Standing tall as Japan's tallest tower, offering stunning views of Tokyo.

About the Tokyo Skytree

The Tokyo Skytree is a broadcasting, restaurant, and observation tower in Sumida, Tokyo, Japan. Completed in 2012, it stands at 634 meters, making it the tallest structure in Japan and the second tallest in the world. The tower offers breathtaking panoramic views of Tokyo and its surroundings.

Things to Do at the Tokyo Skytree

Take in the Views

Enjoy panoramic views of Tokyo from the observation decks of the Skytree.

Dine with a View

Experience unique dining at one of the Skytree’s restaurants with views of Tokyo.

Nighttime Illuminations

Witness the Tokyo Skytree light up in beautiful illuminations every evening.
