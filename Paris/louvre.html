About the Louvre Museum

The Louvre Museum, located on the banks of the River Seine in Paris, France, is home to thousands of works of art, including the iconic Mona Lisa and the Venus de Milo. Originally a royal palace, it was transformed into a museum in 1793. Today, it stands as a symbol of cultural heritage.

Explore the Galleries
