About Montmartre

Montmartre is a historic and artistic district in the north of Paris, France. It has been a hub for artists like Picasso and Van Gogh, offering panoramic views of the city from the Sacré-Cœur Basilica. Its cobbled streets, cafes, and vibrant atmosphere continue to attract both tourists and locals.

Things to Do in Montmartre

Visit the Sacré-Cœur Basilica

Marvel at the beautiful basilica and enjoy the panoramic views of Paris from its dome.

Explore Place du Tertre

Wander the vibrant square where artists display their work, a true piece of Montmartre's artistic history.

Visit the Wall of Love

Discover the "I love you" wall, a romantic spot covered in over 300 languages.
