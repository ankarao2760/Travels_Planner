About the Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is a wrought-iron lattice tower on the Champ de Mars in Paris, France. It was constructed between 1887 and 1889 as the entrance arch to the 1889 World's Fair. Standing at 330 meters, it was the tallest man-made structure in the world until the completion of the Chrysler Building in New York City in 1930.

Things to Do at the Eiffel Tower

Take in the Views

Ascend the tower and enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of Paris.

Dine in Style

Experience fine dining with a view at one of the Eiffel Tower's restaurants.

Nighttime Illuminations

Witness the Eiffel Tower light up in stunning illuminations every evening.
