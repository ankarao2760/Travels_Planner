About Notre Dame Cathedral

Notre Dame Cathedral, also known as Notre-Dame de Paris, is a medieval Catholic cathedral located on the Île de la Cité in the heart of Paris. Built between 1163 and 1345, it is a prime example of French Gothic architecture, renowned for its magnificent façade, stunning stained glass windows, and iconic flying buttresses.

Things to Do at Notre Dame

Explore the Interior

Marvel at the intricate design and stunning stained glass windows inside the cathedral.

Climb the Towers

Ascend the towers for breathtaking views of Paris and a closer look at the famous gargoyles.

Nighttime Views

Experience the cathedral's illuminated beauty at night from the Seine River.
