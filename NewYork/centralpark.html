About Central Park

Central Park, spanning 843 acres, is one of New York City's most iconic landmarks. Established in 1858, it is a lush haven for locals and visitors, featuring walking paths, lakes, meadows, and countless attractions. Whether you're seeking a peaceful retreat or outdoor adventures, Central Park has it all.

Things to Do in Central Park

Row a Boat

Rent a rowboat at the Loeb Boathouse and glide through the park's picturesque lake.

Visit Belvedere Castle

Explore this historic landmark with stunning views of the park.

Ride the Carousel

Enjoy a nostalgic ride on the famous Central Park Carousel, loved by children and adults alike.
