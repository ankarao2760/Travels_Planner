About the Statue of Liberty

The Statue of Liberty, a gift from France to the United States, stands proudly on Liberty Island in New York Harbor. Dedicated on October 28, 1886, it symbolizes freedom, democracy, and hope. With its 305-foot height and torch of enlightenment, it welcomes millions of visitors annually.

Things to Do at the Statue of Liberty

Ferry Ride

Take a scenic ferry ride to Liberty Island with views of Manhattan's skyline.

Climb to the Crown

Ascend the spiral staircase to the crown for a breathtaking view of New York Harbor.

Liberty Museum

Explore the interactive exhibits at the Statue of Liberty Museum.
