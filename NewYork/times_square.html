About Times Square

Times Square is one of the most iconic landmarks in New York City. Known for its dazzling lights, towering billboards, and non-stop energy, it attracts millions of visitors annually. From Broadway theaters to world-class dining and shopping, Times Square offers an unforgettable experience at the heart of Manhattan.

Things to Do in Times Square

Catch a Broadway Show

Enjoy world-famous musicals and plays at one of the many nearby theaters.

Neon Nightlife

Marvel at the vibrant LED billboards lighting up the square at night.

New Year’s Eve Ball Drop

Join the iconic countdown and celebration on New Year's Eve.
