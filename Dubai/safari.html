About Dubai Desert Safari

Dubai Desert Safari offers a thrilling adventure across the beautiful dunes of the Arabian Desert. Whether you're looking for a calm ride in the desert or an adrenaline-packed dune bashing experience, the desert safari has something for everyone.

Things to Do in a Desert Safari

Dune Bashing

Feel the adrenaline rush while riding across the desert dunes in a 4x4 vehicle.

Camel Riding

Experience a traditional camel ride through the desert landscape.

Sandboarding

Try sandboarding down the towering dunes for a unique desert experience.
