About Palm Jumeirah

Palm Jumeirah is a man-made island in the shape of a palm tree, located off the coast of Dubai. Known for its luxury resorts, upscale homes, and stunning views, Palm Jumeirah is a symbol of Dubai's bold vision for innovation and development.

Things to Do at Palm Jumeirah

Stay at Atlantis

Experience luxury at the famous Atlantis, The Palm Resort, offering world-class amenities and entertainment.

Relax on Private Beaches

Indulge in a relaxing day at the private beaches surrounding Palm Jumeirah.

Shopping and Dining

Explore the exclusive shopping malls and enjoy fine dining with incredible views.
