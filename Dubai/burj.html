About Burj Khalifa

The Burj Khalifa is a mega tall skyscraper in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. It stands at a height of 828 meters, making it the tallest man-made structure in the world. Completed in 2010, it is an architectural marvel and an integral part of Dubai's skyline.

Things to Do at Burj Khalifa

At the Top Observation Deck

Enjoy stunning 360-degree views of Dubai from the observation decks on the 124th and 148th floors.

Dine in Luxury

Experience world-class dining at the Burj Khalifa's exclusive restaurants.

Night Light Show

Witness the dazzling light show and fountain displays at the base of the Burj Khalifa.
