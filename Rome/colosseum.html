About the Colosseum

The Colosseum, also known as the Flavian Amphitheatre, is a large ancient amphitheater located in the center of Rome. It is one of the greatest works of Roman architecture and engineering, famous for gladiatorial games and other public spectacles.

Walk through the historic arena where gladiators once fought.

Roman Ruins

Discover the ancient ruins around the Colosseum for a complete historical experience.
