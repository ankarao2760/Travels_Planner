About the Trevi Fountain

The Trevi Fountain is a monumental Baroque fountain in Rome, famous for its magnificent sculptures and the tradition of throwing coins into the water.

Things to Do at Trevi Fountain

Coin Tossing

Follow the tradition and toss a coin into the fountain to ensure your return to Rome.

Photography

Capture the beauty of the Trevi Fountain from various angles.

Evening Illuminations

Experience the magical atmosphere of the fountain lit up at night.
